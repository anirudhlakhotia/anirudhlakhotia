### Anirudh Lakhotia

Software Engineer at Couchbase, where I build SDKs and connectors for production
data systems.

I work on information retrieval and efficient machine learning. I am particularly
interested in how representations behave under practical constraints.
Representations are more than model internals. They also have to be cheap to
store and fast to serve at query time.

#### Research

[Most MaxSim Winners Flip, Retrieval Survives: Low-Margin Substitution in Sign-Coded Late Interaction](https://github.com/anirudhlakhotia/subbit-multivector-retrieval)  
ReNeuIR @ SIGIR 2026. Sign coding flips most token-level MaxSim winners, yet
retrieval survives because the substitutes are low-margin. The linked repository
contains the paper and full reproduction recipe.

[UnoLoRA: Single Low-Rank Adaptation for Efficient Multitask Fine-tuning](https://openreview.net/forum?id=n6W0QkQBgw)  
FITML @ NeurIPS 2024. One shared low-rank adapter supports multitask fine-tuning
without duplicating a separate adapter for every task.

[Analysis of Sampling Strategies for Multi-Task Learning in Transformer Models](https://anirudhlakhotia.com/publications/#lakhotia2025sampling)  
ICEENG 2025. Task-sampling strategies produce different internal representations
even when aggregate multitask performance is similar.

I am also working on compositionality in learned representations, and how to
separate learned structure from dataset-level effects.
